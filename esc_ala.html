02 / THE PROJECT

MORE THAN
A STORE.

ESC_ALA started as an opportunity to explore what a modern bouldering gym brand could look and feel like through a fully customized Shopify experience.

Rather than relying on a standard storefront layout, I developed a visual direction and custom theme that brings together bold typography, expressive color, motion, and flexible content sections.
