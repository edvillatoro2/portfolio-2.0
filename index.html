  • A LITTLE ABOUT ME — 03

    About.

    I'm a front-end developer with 5+ years of experience in Vue, Nuxt and TypeScript, building accessible, high-performance interfaces for high-traffic booking platforms. I turn complex Figma designs into WCAG-compliant pages, build reusable Storybook component libraries, and keep production steady with Jest testing and cross-browser QA.

    I also have a designer's eye, and I'm expanding it in two directions: building custom Shopify themes in Liquid, and studying graphic design at ilovecreatives (Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop). I like the point where a brand's visual identity meets a storefront that is fast, clear and easy to use. Outside of work, I boulder and collect design inspiration.
